Look

Black is see-through on the glasses. Invert turns a white desktop into glowing text on a clear background and saves battery.

Line up your eyes

Adjust until the two images merge into one. Each change reconnects the glasses for a few seconds. Every other glasses program uses this too.

Battery saver

Keep "off in the case" on: a lit display stops the arms from charging. "Look up to see" shows the screen only when you tilt your head up by that many degrees (0 = always on), which saves the most battery.

Top bar & controls

Pause clears the lenses. Reset puts the picture back to the default spot and size (keeps brightness and eye alignment).
